Site Plan: Digital Minds Coding Academy

Prepared by: Aniwen Blessing | Founder, Digital Minds Coding Academy

Site Name

Name: Digital Minds Coding Academy

Reason: This name represents an academy focused on empowering individuals all over the country, and beyond with technology, coding, and creation skills through initiatives like 'Digital Mind Crews'. It clearly communicates the educational and technological focus of the platform.

Optional Domain Availability: digitalmindsacademy.org

Site Purpose

The site serves as a central hub for aspiring developers and tech enthusiasts. It provides detailed information about available courses, a dedicated mentorship section, success stories of past students, and a registration form (powered by Formspree) that collects essential user details, including phone numbers, to facilitate direct communication and enrollment.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "I am a complete beginner in Lagos. What coding courses do you offer, and how can I register and pay for them?"
  • Scenario 2: "I want to improve my skills with guidance. How can I get matched with a mentor through the Digital Mind Crews initiative, and what are the success stories of past students?"

Color Schema

The following colors are actively applied to this document to demonstrate the schema:

  • Deep Blue (#1A365D): Used for headings, footer background, and primary branding elements to convey trust and professionalism.
  • Vibrant Orange (#ED8936): Used for accents, links, and call-to-action buttons (like the registration button) to draw attention and inspire energy.
  • Off-White (#F7FAFC): Used for the main page background to ensure high readability and a clean, modern look.
  • Dark Gray (#2D3748): Used for paragraph text for optimal contrast and readability.

Typography

  • Poppins (Sans-Serif): Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3). It provides a modern, clean, and professional appearance suitable for a tech academy.
  • Open Sans (Sans-Serif): Used for all body text and paragraphs. It is highly legible on both mobile and desktop screens, ensuring a good user experience.
